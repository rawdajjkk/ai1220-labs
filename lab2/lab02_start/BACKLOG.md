# Backlog — LabLoans

LabLoans is a system that helps the lab keep track of borrowed equipment and make sure it is returned.

## Items
- [F] Borrowers need to receive a reminder before equipment becomes overdue. (assistant)
- [F] Lab staff need to record the condition of equipment when it is returned. (assistant)
## Items

- [F] Lab staff need to record which equipment has been borrowed and who borrowed it.
- [F] Lab staff need a weekly report of every open loan.
- [F] Lab staff need to see which equipment is currently available or on loan.
- [F] Borrowers need to know when their borrowed equipment is due for return.
- [F] Borrowers need to receive a reminder before equipment becomes overdue. (assistant)
- [F] Lab staff need to identify equipment that has not been returned on time.
- [F] Borrowers need to confirm when equipment has been returned.
- [F] Lab staff need to record the condition of equipment when it is returned. (assistant)
- [NF] Borrower information must only be accessible to authorized lab staff.
- [NF] The system should show updated equipment availability quickly after a loan or return is recorded.
- [NF] The system should be simple enough for lab staff to use without special training.

## The change

The client requested a weekly report of every open loan because the audit showed that most equipment goes missing during exam weeks.

I placed this item second in the backlog because exams start soon and the report is now a high-priority need.

The weekly open-loan report was added to Sprint 1. The item about borrowers knowing their return due date was removed from Sprint 1 so that the sprint does not grow.

## From the assistant

The assistant suggested these five backlog items:

1. [F] Borrowers need to receive a reminder before equipment becomes overdue.
   - Kept because reminders could reduce the chance of equipment being returned late.

2. [F] Lab staff need to record the condition of equipment when it is returned.
   - Kept because this helps staff keep accurate records of returned equipment.

3. [F] Lab staff need to see a history of previous loans for each piece of equipment.
   - Rejected because the current priority is tracking open and overdue loans.

4. [NF] The system should allow authorized staff to access loan information quickly.
   - Rejected because the backlog already includes a requirement for equipment information to update quickly.

5. [NF] Borrower information should remain private and secure.
   - Rejected because the backlog already includes a privacy requirement for borrower information.
