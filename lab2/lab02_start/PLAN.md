# Plan — <pLabLoans>

## Process choice
The requirements are not very stable because the client only said that the lab keeps losing equipment. More details may be added after getting feedback from the lab staff.

### How stable and binding are the requirements?
The requirements are not very stable because the client only said that the lab keeps losing equipment. More details may be added after getting feedback from the lab staff.

### How quickly can real feedback arrive?
Feedback can arrive quickly because lab staff can test the system and explain what works or needs to change.

### What does failure cost?
Failure could cause equipment to be lost or records to be incorrect. This could cost the lab money and make it difficult to know who has borrowed equipment.

### How many pieces must move together?
The system can be developed in small pieces. Loan recording, equipment availability, due dates, and overdue items can be added step by step.

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Lab staff may forget to record a loan or return. | Medium | Make the loan and return process simple and quick to complete. |
| Borrower information could be seen by unauthorized people. | Low | Allow only authorized lab staff to access borrower information. |
| Requirements may change after staff test the system. | Medium | Work in short increments and use feedback to update the backlog. |
