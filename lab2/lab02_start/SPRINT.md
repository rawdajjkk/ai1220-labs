# Sprint 1 — LabLoans

## Sprint Goal

By the end of Sprint 1, lab staff can record equipment loans, see equipment status, and review a weekly report of all open loans.

## Selected items

- [F] Lab staff need to record which equipment has been borrowed and who borrowed it.
- [F] Lab staff need a weekly report of every open loan.
- [F] Lab staff need to see which equipment is currently available or on loan.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Lab staff can record a loan with the equipment and borrower information.
- [ ] Lab staff can see whether each piece of equipment is available or on loan.
- [ ] Lab staff can view a weekly report containing every open loan.
- [ ] Another person can test all selected Sprint 1 requirements without asking the developer for help.
